# SaaS-Sales-Pipeline-Conversion-Optimization
🏢 Business Context

TechnoServe (SaaS startup) experienced a drop in sales conversion from 35% to 25%, directly impacting revenue growth.

🎯 Problem Statement

Why is the sales pipeline conversion declining, and how can we increase it?

🧠 Hypotheses Tested

H1: Lead quality degradation

H2: Poor early-stage qualification

H3: Long sales cycle reduces closure probability

H4: Certain industries underperform

H5: Budget impacts conversion likelihood
